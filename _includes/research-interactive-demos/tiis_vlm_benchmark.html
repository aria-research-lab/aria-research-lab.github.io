Paper Finding (LLaVA-NeXT-Video): Human feedback substantially improves verification accuracy over the No Feedback Model, surging from 40.0% up to 90.0% (+50 percentage points) with Free-Text feedback. Even lightweight binary feedback elevates model accuracy to 80.0% (+40 percentage points), demonstrating that modern video-language models effectively ground corrective human signals across interaction granularities.
