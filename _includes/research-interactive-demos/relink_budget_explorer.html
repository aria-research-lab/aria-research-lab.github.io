1. What is the Problem? Influence Maximization in Closed Social Networks (IM-CSN)
Conceptual Problem Formulation • Paper Figure 1 (k = 2)

Problem Formulation: In closed social networks, content propagation is restricted across privacy boundaries. Starting from a seed node, the goal is to choose closed candidate edges to activate subject to an edge activation budget k (where k is the maximum number of candidate edges that may be activated) to maximize total influence spread under the Independent Cascade (IC) diffusion model.

Clarification: This example illustrates the sequential optimization problem (Example 1, Figure 1 from the CIKM 2025 paper). It does not represent RELINK’s learned edge-selection policy.

In a closed social network, content propagation is constrained. The process begins with seed node a (red). Its outgoing links (a→b, a→h, a→d, a→c) form the initial set of candidate edges (red arrows). Dormant links deeper in the network remain inactive (dark arrows). The edge activation budget k = 2 allows at most 2 candidate edges to be activated from node a.
